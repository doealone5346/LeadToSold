Why do I need a website? I'm already on the portals.

“Why do I need a website? I'm already on Zillow and my CRM.”

Portals give you visibility. A website gives you ownership.

A listing portal can show your name, but it can't protect your business. On a platform you're borrowing attention — your profile sits next to competing agents, the same lead can be sold more than once, and your ranking can shift whenever the platform changes its rules.

Your website works differently. It's the one place online where you own the whole thing — your name, your reviews, your listings, and your leads. No competing agents on the page. No surprise algorithm changes. No platform taking a cut of your growth.

Portals help people find an agent. Your website helps them choose you. That's the difference between being listed and being established.

01

The 90-Day Guarantee

All LeadToSold lead plans — Sold Starter, Sold Pro, and Sold Dominator — include a 90-day money-back guarantee starting from the date your onboarding form is submitted.

If, within those 90 days, we have not delivered verified leads matching the criteria you gave us at onboarding, you may request a full refund of your one-time plan payment.

The verified-lead volume committed under each plan is:

  • Sold Starter — 25–30 verified leads across 3–5 target zip codes (leads only — appointment scheduling is not included)
  • Sold Pro — 40–45 verified leads across 6–10 target zip codes or 3–5 counties
  • Sold Dominator — 60–65 verified leads across unlimited zip codes, counties and cities
02

What Qualifies for a Refund

You are eligible for a full refund if any of the following apply within the guarantee window:

  • Non-delivery. We did not deliver the verified-lead volume stated in your plan.
  • Wrong criteria. Leads consistently fell outside the zip codes, counties, price range, or lead types you specified at onboarding.
  • Unverified contacts. A significant share of delivered leads had invalid phone numbers or email addresses that we were unable to replace.
  • Duplicate or shared leads. A lead you received was also sold to another agent, contrary to our exclusivity promise.
  • Missing plan features. Scheduled appointments (Sold Pro and Sold Dominator) or live transfers (Sold Dominator) were included in your plan but not provided.
  • Service not started. You paid but we were unable to begin service for reasons on our side.
03

What Doesn't Qualify

To keep the guarantee fair and sustainable, refunds are not available in these situations:

  • Leads delivered but not contacted. We can verify intent and provide contact details, but we cannot close deals on your behalf. Leads must be worked.
  • No closings. A verified, interested lead is what we sell. The closing figures shown on our pricing page (for example, “expect 3–4 closings on average”) are averages across accounts, not a guaranteed outcome. Whether a deal closes depends on your market, pricing, and follow-up.
  • Appointment setting on Sold Starter. Sold Starter is a leads-only plan. Appointments are not part of that plan, so their absence is not grounds for a refund — Sold Pro and Sold Dominator include scheduling.
